Course Overview & Learning Outcomes

Mandated under the National Statistical Systems Training Framework (2024-25)

Field investigators across NSSO and State DES Directorates require precise operational capability to construct multi-stage probabilistic samples. This comprehensive course prepares civil servants to execute complex sampling allocations under rigorous GIGW and MoSPI methodological standards, ensuring survey estimates achieve target coefficients of variation with statistical reliability.

verified_user Verified Cadre Capabilities (MoSPI Competency Framework)

  • check_circle Master First Stage Unit (FSU) selection: Execute robust Probability Proportional to Size with Replacement (PPSWR) using Census enumeration blocks and village frames.
  • check_circle Design Effects & Intraclass Correlation: Calculate complex design effects (deff) and rate of homogeneity (roh) for NSSO multi-stage stratified clusters.
  • check_circle Unequal Probability Estimation: Apply Hansen-Hurwitz and Horvitz-Thompson estimators to eliminate non-response bias in high-variance agricultural and manufacturing sectors.
  • check_circle Audit Urban Frame Survey (UFS): Detect systematic periodicity anomalies, boundaries erosion, and random start slippage in municipal ward subdivisions.
  • check_circle Second-Stage Unit (SSU) Stratification: Validate household listing schedules, MPCE ranking bands, and sub-stratification structures for Computer-Assisted Personal Interviewing (CAPI) tablets.
