## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-trust, authoritative, and data-dense digital platform serving India's Official Statistical System under the Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI), operating as a specialized AI-powered capacity-building layer within the broader iGOT Karmayogi civil services ecosystem.

The visual direction follows a **Corporate / Modern Government Digital Service** philosophy: structured, accessible, high-legibility, and institutional yet distinctly dynamic. The design system unites the sovereign dignity of the Government of India with state-of-the-art adaptive learning paradigms (spaced repetition, topological dependency trees, and competency radar frameworks).

### Core Attributes
- **Authoritative & Sovereign:** Anchor tones derived from deep navy hues communicate constitutional permanence, procedural integrity, and rigorous governance.
- **Dynamic & Energizing:** Warm saffron and luminous ochre accents derived from the brand insignia introduce purposeful urgency, highlighting actionable training interventions, review cadences, and milestone achievements.
- **Data-Dense & Legible:** Clean card surfaces, crisp structural dividing lines, and tabular numeral typesetting present complex statistical metrics, matrices, and distributions with minimal cognitive friction.
- **Empathetic & Accessible:** Adheres strictly to GIGW (Guidelines for Indian Government Websites) and WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA compliance across typography contrast, screen readers, and multi-script localization.

## Colors

The color palette reflects official institutional weight counterbalanced by purposeful learning telemetry.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Navy (`#1B365D` / `#1B4CA1`):** The foundational color used for persistent global navigation, hero section gradients, table headers, and primary administrative typography.
- **Action Saffron & Karmayogi Orange (`#F47920` / `#FFA730`):** Applied strictly to primary actions, interactive steps, active navigation indicators, and urgent revision alert buttons.
- **Canvas & Card Surfaces (`#F4F6F9` & `#FFFFFF`):** An airy, neutral backdrop paired with clean white elevated surfaces creates high signal-to-noise clarity across high-density dashboards.
- **Sovereign Accent:** A 3-stop 3px hairline tricolor stripe (`#FF9933`, `#FFFFFF`, `#138808`) sits permanently at the very top edge of the browser viewport to ground the platform in national service.

### Semantic Status Tokens
- **Mastered / Met (`#1B873F`):** Competency criteria achieved, assessment passed, or node fully certified.
- **In Progress / Medium Gap (`#D97706`):** Active learning trajectory, moderate prerequisite requirement, or nearing decay threshold.
- **Immediate Need / High Gap (`#C2410C`):** Urgent competency deficit identified by the matrix, or critical SM-2 memory decay requiring intervention.

### Domain Color Coding
- `statistical`: Institutional Cobalt (`#1B4CA1`)
- `technical`: Teal Slate (`#0D9488`)
- `digital_governance`: Deep Amethyst (`#7C3AED`)
- `behavioural`: Warm Amber (`#D97706`)

## Typography

The type system pairs **Noto Sans** for display and narrative body text with **Inter** for data surfaces, interactive labels, and numerical indicators.

### Key Rules
- **Multilingual Readiness:** Noto Sans natively bridges Devanagari (Hindi) and Latin scripts, preserving optical x-height and vertical rhythm across bilingual government views.
- **Tabular Numerals:** All metric strips, statistical percentages, completion gauges, and table figures must enable font-feature settings `"tnum"` (tabular numbers) and `"cv02"` / `"cv04"` for ambiguity-free numeral differentiation.
- **Information Hierarchy:** Section headers use bold weights with reduced letter spacing, immediately followed by 14px muted body descriptions to anchor data density without visual clutter.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model employs a responsive 12-column grid system built on an 8px base rhythm, prioritizing predictable vertical alignment across analytical cards.

### Layout Specs
- **Maximum Width:** Global container constrains at `1280px` (`80rem`) centered, preventing ultra-wide line measures on high-resolution office workstations.
- **Gutters & Margins:**
  - Desktop (≥1024px): 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters with 32px (`2rem`) outer margin.
  - Tablet (768px–1023px): 16px (`1rem`) gutters with 24px (`1.5rem`) outer margin.
  - Mobile (<768px): 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters with 16px (`1rem`) outer margin.
- **Top Utility Stack:**
  1. Top Hairline Bar: 3px sovereign tricolor strip.
  2. Accessibility Sub-Bar: 36px height (`#0F172A` background or neutral light tint) for font resizer, contrast toggle, and official seals.
  3. Primary Header: 72px fixed height housing the co-branded logo lockup, search, and officer profile.
  4. Primary Tab Navigation: 48px persistent horizontal bar with bottom-border saffron active indicator.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids excessive blur or skeuomorphic drops, maintaining an authentic, clean Indian government portal standard with crisp separation.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas):** `#F4F6F9` slate background providing comfortable ambient contrast against white content panels.
- **Tier 1 (Base Cards & Modules):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a subtle 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Modules):** Shift up on hover with `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.04)` and border shift to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Context Overlays & Flyout Menus):** `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` with a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`) over a 50% opacity navy scrim (`rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.5)`).
- **Tier 4 (Hero Gradient Surface):** Linear gradient from `#1B365D` (Navy Prime) to `#0B5C9E` (Deep Azure) with a 6% subtle floral/geometric emblem pattern overlay.

## Shapes

The design system uses a balanced roundedness profile (`roundedness: 2`, where base elements feature 8px to 10px radius), balancing institutional gravity with modern software friendliness.

### Shape Geometry Rules
- **Base Cards & Panels:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) to `10px` (`0.625rem`) corner radius.
- **Buttons & Input Fields:** `6px` (`0.375rem`) for compact enterprise utility, up to `8px` for primary hero callouts.
- **Badges & Micro Chips:** `4px` (`0.25rem`) for square-cornered metadata badges; full pill (`9999px`) reserved specifically for status badges (e.g., Mastered, Gap Alert) and numerical count counters.
- **Icon Enclosures:** Rounded squircle (`8px`) or circle (`50%`) with low-saturation colored background tints.

- **GIGW Accessibility Strip:** Sits directly beneath the tricolor band. Contains:
  - India Tricolor icon and "Government of India / भारत सरकार" text.
  - Accessibility tools: font-scaling controls `A-` | `A` | `A+`, High-Contrast toggle, and Language Switcher (English | हिन्दी).
- **Global Header (White Background, 72px):**
  - **Left Brand Lockup:** DIKSHA insignia emblem (golden lotus & digital nib motif) sized at 44px height. Border divider (1px `#CBD5E1`, 32px height). Accompanying typography: **DIKSHA** in bold navy text, with sub-line: *"Adaptive Skill Intelligence • MoSPI & iGOT Karmayogi"*. Beside it, the National Emblem Ashoka Lion watermark with *"Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation"*.
  - **Center Search:** Universal search input with keyboard shortcut tooltip (`Ctrl + K`) and category filter for competency nodes vs. circulars.
  - **Right Profile Group:** Notification bell with badge counter for due SM-2 flashcard reviews, followed by user avatar capsule displaying the officer's photo, name ("Rajesh Kumar"), and cadre subtitle ("Statistical Investigator Gr. II").
- **Sub-Nav Bar (White, 48px):**
  - Horizontal items: My Dashboard, Competency Passport, Learning Roadmap, Assessments & Quiz, Knowledge Repository, MDO Workforce Analytics.
  - Active tab receives a solid 3px border-bottom in Karmayogi Orange (`#F47920`) with font weight 600 in navy (`#1B365D`).

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#F47920`, text `#FFFFFF`, bold weight. Subtle hover transition to `#E0630D`. Focus state features a 2px offset outline in `#1B4CA1`.
- **Institutional Secondary:** Background `#1B4CA1`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover to `#163E85`.
- **Outline / Portal Action:** Border 1px `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1B365D`. Hover brings `#F8FAFC` background and `#0F172A` text.
- **Destructive / Flag:** Background `#FEE2E2`, border 1px `#FCA5A5`, text `#C2410C`.

### 3. Cards & Container Surfaces
- **Metrics Strip Cards:** White surface, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, 8px radius. Contains a top domain icon, a large metric display (32px tabular), and a label with trend indication.
- **Course & Module Cards:**
  - Header with official iGOT provider tag ("MoSPI Training Division • iGOT Karmayogi").
  - Course title in 16px semi-bold Navy text.
  - Duration indicator ("⏱️ 16 hours") and prerequisite pill.
  - Bottom action bar with direct enrollment button and competency delta tag (`+12% Statistical Theory`).

### 4. Specialized Dashboard Components
- **SM-2 Forgetting Curve Cadence Banner:**
  - Highlighted surface in soft amber/orange tint (`#FFFBEB`) with left accent border (4px `#F47920`).
  - Displays icon representing spaced repetition cadence.
  - Text: node name (e.g., *"Python for Data Analysis"*), decay rationale (*"Review overdue by 3 days • 30% retention risk"*), and action button (*"Take 5-min Booster Quiz"*).
- **Topological Learning Dependency Stepper (Kahn's Order):**
  - Visual node sequence linked by directional connector tracks.
  - Node States:
    - *Completed:* Green badge with checkmark (`#1B873F`).
    - *Current / Unlocked:* Navy outline with glowing saffron ring.
    - *Locked:* Gray border with padlock icon.
  - Each node shows: Module Name, Domain Pill, Level step (`Basic ➔ Intermediate`), and Gap Severity badge (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`).
- **4-Domain Competency Radar & Progress Meters:**
  - Categorized into Statistical, Technical, Digital Governance, Behavioural.
  - Stacked 4-segment progress bar with discrete step pips representing: `None` (0%), `Basic` (25%), `Intermediate` (50%), `Advanced` (75%), and `Master` (100%).
- **Assessment Radio Cards:**
  - Full-width selectable card surfaces with 1px border `#E2E8F0` and radio button indicator.
  - On hover: `#F1F5F9` background.
  - Selected state: `#EFF6FF` background, 2px border `#1B4CA1`.
  - Review state: Correct answer tinted `#DCFCE7` with green checkmark; incorrect answer tinted `#FEE2E2` with cross mark and rationale text block.

### 5. Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 40px, padding 8px 12px, border 1px `#CBD5E1`, border-radius 6px, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Focused: border `#1B4CA1`, ring 3px `rgba(27, 76, 161, 0.15)`.
- **Government Document Uploader (Knowledge Hub):**
  - Dashed border zone (2px `#94A3B8`, dash array `6, 6`), `#F8FAFC` background.
  - Supports PDF, DOCX, TXT with file size caps (up to 50MB) and e-Office metadata auto-parsing tags.
